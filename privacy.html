Privacy Policy

Personal Backup Utility is a personal-use application operated only by its owner.

It accesses storage authorized by the owner only for backup-related operations, including listing, uploading, downloading, and restoring the owner's own backup files.

User data is not sold, used for advertising, or used for profiling. The utility does not distribute user data to third parties.

This public site does not publish backup contents or access credentials. It has no forms, analytics, advertising, external embeds, or application-specific tracking cookies.
